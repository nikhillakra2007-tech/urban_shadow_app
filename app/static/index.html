About Urban Shadow

An urban-grid scoring platform for NCT Delhi built on a 500m × 500m grid of 6,284 cells. Each cell aggregates 18 geospatial metric groups and produces 11 normalized scores.

Score Composition (weights)

  • Heat 15%
  • Environment (NDVI + parks) 15%
  • Walkability 10%
  • Traffic 10%
  • Flood Risk 10%
  • Air Quality 10%
  • Accessibility 10%
  • Transit 10%
  • Services 10%

Usability Classes

High ≥ 75 Moderate ≥ 50 Low ≥ 25 Very Low < 25

Data Sources

Landsat LST, Sentinel-2 NDVI, WorldPop, OpenStreetMap (roads, buildings, services, transit), PM2.5/PM10 stations, WorldCover, and more — imported from the project's Google Drive dataset.
